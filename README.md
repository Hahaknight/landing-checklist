# The Landing Page Checklist

**23 checks to run before you ship a landing page — tick them off and see your score.**

🔗 **Use it online: <https://hahaknight.github.io/landing-checklist/>**

## Why this exists

Most landing pages don't fail on design — they fail on the boring stuff: a hero that doesn't answer "what is this", pricing that creates fear instead of clarity, trust signals buried in the footer. This checklist walks the page top-to-bottom and tells you which of those gaps you still have.

## The 23 checks, grouped by section

- **Hero (the 5-second test)** — can a stranger tell what you do, for whom, in 5 seconds?
- **Proof (borrowed trust)** — testimonials, logos, numbers: is any real evidence above the fold?
- **Body (the skim path)** — does the page survive being skimmed, not read?
- **Pricing (the fear zone)** — are plans, limits and the free tier unambiguous?
- **Trust (the boring stuff that closes)** — refund policy, contact, FAQ, legal links
- **Mobile & mechanics** — thumb reach, tap targets, load, forms
- …plus the remaining checks in the tool

Check items off as you fix them; the score counter shows how close to shippable you are.

## Self-host

# open index.html directly, or enable GitHub Pages on your fork
